I work on solar PV, battery storage, and their integration into power systems, mostly in Southeast Asia. My work covers feasibility studies, grid integration and ancillary services, power sector regulation and tariffs, and training for utilities and regulators.

I am a Scientist at the Qatar Environment and Energy Research Institute (QEERI), Hamad Bin Khalifa University, where I am a principal investigator for projects on resilient PV systems for arid environments and on grid technologies. My work there covers PV field performance in arid conditions, inverter reliability, and the techno-economics and life-cycle assessment of PV systems.

Before that, I spent eleven years at the Solar Energy Research Institute of Singapore (SERIS), National University of Singapore. There I carried out feasibility studies for more than 7 GWp of PV and 1 GWh of battery storage, led the PV grid integration working group of a solar forecasting programme for the Energy Market Authority and Singapore Power, and maintained the SERIS Global Floating PV Database (more than 1,700 projects).

In Indonesia, I have worked with GIZ and the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources on ancillary services, the regulatory framework for battery storage, rooftop PV hosting capacity, and PV and battery tariffs, and with PLN on ancillary services, demand response, and renewable energy forecasting. Other studies were funded by ADB, the World Bank, the Inter-American Development Bank, and USAID, in countries including the Philippines, Viet Nam, Sri Lanka, Mexico, Tuvalu, and Suriname. I also co-founded Alva Energi, which has installed more than 800 kWp of solar PV across 18 cities and regencies in Indonesia.

I work in English, Indonesian, and Malay.
