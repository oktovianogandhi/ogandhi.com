I have worked across the project cycle of solar photovoltaic (PV) and battery storage projects: global screening and market data, pre-feasibility and feasibility studies, regulation and tariffs, installation, and training for the people who will operate the systems. Most of this work has been in Southeast Asia, with feasibility studies in South Asia, the Pacific, and Latin America.

## Feasibility and options studies

- **Floating PV for development banks, 2021 to 2026.** Floating PV expert on teams led by Dornier Suntrace and Trama TecnoAmbiental (TTA):
    - Philippines: floating solar project technical support under the Asian Development Bank's (ADB) TA-9292, Strengthening Project Preparation Capacity in Asia and the Pacific (ADB, 2021 to 2022);
    - Suriname: feasibility studies of floating solar PV (Inter-American Development Bank (IDB), 2022 to 2023);
    - Sri Lanka: feasibility studies and environmental and social impact assessment of 30 to 50 MWp floating solar power plants (United States Agency for International Development (USAID), 2022 to 2023);
    - Mexico: options study for floating PV facilities (World Bank, 2024);
    - Tuvalu: feasibility study of lagoon floating PV (World Bank, 2024);
    - Viet Nam: exploring renewable energy hybrid facilities, including floating PV with hydropower (World Bank, 2025 to 2026).

    The work covered technology selection, energy yield assessment, layout and preliminary engineering design, economic viability, grid interconnection and permitting, and implementation and ownership models.

- **PV and battery storage for developers and investors, 2022 to 2026.** Pre-feasibility and feasibility studies in Indonesia, Norway, Pakistan, and Singapore. Together with the studies above, more than 7 GWp of PV and more than 1 GWh of battery storage assessed.
- **Decarbonisation of a gas pipeline network, Indonesia, 2024.** Project leader of a pre-feasibility study for a gas transmission company: rooftop and ground-mounted PV, battery storage, and hydrogen electrolysers across more than 1,000 km of pipeline and 20 facilities, with regulation, economic viability, financing options, carbon dioxide reduction, and risks.

## Power sector regulation, planning, and advisory

- **GIZ Energy Programme Indonesia, since December 2023.** Expert on the Renewable Energy for Electrification Programme (REEP2), Clean, Affordable and Secure Energy for Southeast Asia (CASE), and Green Energy projects of the Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ), advising Indonesia's Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources (MEMR) and the state electricity company, PT PLN (Persero), through GIZ:
    - one of three main authors, with Energynautics and UMBRA, of a comparative study of ancillary services for Indonesia, with workshops for MEMR, Bappenas, and PLN;
    - one of three main authors of a regulatory framework and business model for battery energy storage systems (BESS), including the power purchase agreement (PPA) price analysis for PV and wind with storage;
    - a financial model for MEMR to test draft benchmark prices for utility-scale PV, PV with battery storage, and stand-alone battery storage, reporting the levelised cost of electricity (LCOE), project and equity returns, and debt service coverage under different financing terms;
    - for a carbon finance programme, estimates of the LCOE of floating PV at candidate reservoir sites and of the carbon price needed to close the gap to Indonesia's ceiling tariff;
    - advice on rooftop PV hosting capacity and quotas, battery storage, floating PV, demand response, variable renewable energy forecasting, and power system expansion planning;
    - quality assurance of outputs by national and international consultants.
- **Singapore's Energy Market Authority (EMA) and Singapore Power (SP), 2019 to 2022.** Leader of the PV grid integration working group in a national solar forecasting programme: network modelling; the effect of distributed PV and storage on adequacy, stability, reserves, generation mix, and prices; a comparison of integration measures; and the value of solar forecasts. Findings presented to EMA and SP.
- **National Research Foundation (NRF) Singapore and EMA, 2015 to 2019.** Researcher on power grid stability with an increasing share of renewables: the techno-economics of reactive power from PV, the cost of inverter wear, and the effect of distributed PV and storage on stability and the market.

## Island and off-grid systems

- **Diesel replacement on an industrial island grid, Riau Islands, Indonesia, 2015 to 2018.** Researcher for NRF Singapore and an industrial client: site visits, load measurements, a control and energy management framework for a 24 MVA isolated network, and a roadmap for PV siting, battery sizing, and phased diesel replacement, tested with hardware in the loop.
- **Rural electrification, Southwest Sumba, East Nusa Tenggara, 2018 to 2019.** Through Alva Energi, with a student community programme: two communal off-grid PV systems serving 42 households in the village of Homba Karipit. I helped design the systems, procure the equipment, install them, and train the local operator.
- **Energy roadmap and off-grid PV, North Sumatra, 2018 to 2020.** Project manager for an orangutan sanctuary: energy needs analysis, an energy roadmap, and the planning and installation of grid-connected and off-grid PV.

## Training and capacity building

- **PT PLN (Persero), MEMR, and Bappenas, since 2023.** Curricula, training materials, and delivery on power system expansion planning with variable renewable energy (including a training of trainers with PLN Academy), ancillary services, battery storage, demand response, pumped storage hydropower, PV power plants, variable renewable energy forecasting, and long-term electricity planning.
- **Off-grid PV in Eastern Indonesia, 2022 to 2023.** Team leader for Pattimura University in Ambon, funded by New Zealand's NZMATES programme: training needs assessment, curricula for a mini-grid laboratory and a university PV course, training of six trainers, and a roadmap for a solar training centre.
- **National University of Singapore, 2015 to 2021.** Undergraduate and graduate teaching on renewable energy, physics, and power systems, and project supervision.

## Company

- **[Alva Energi](https://alvaenergi.com/), Indonesia, since 2017.** Co-founder of a solar PV engineering, procurement, and construction (EPC) and consultancy company. Alva Energi helps homes, businesses, and industry in Indonesia cut their electricity costs and carbon footprint with rooftop PV, brings off-grid PV to remote villages, and advises companies and institutions on decarbonisation, including the pipeline network study above. My work there covers project management and supervision, design and budget review, training of engineers, licensing and net metering with PLN, and business models and financing with banks and developers.

- **Solar Energy Research Institute of Singapore (SERIS), National University of Singapore, 2015 to 2026.** Research Scholar, then Research Fellow (2019) and Senior Research Fellow (2024).
