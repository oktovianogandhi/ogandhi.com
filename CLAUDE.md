# ogandhi.com: notes for Claude

Okto Gandhi's personal website. Claude builds and maintains it; Okto approves content and every publish.

- Build: `pip install markdown` (if missing), then `python3 build.py` (Python 3.9+; keep f-string expressions free of backslashes). It rewrites `docs/`. Always rebuild and commit `docs/` together with source changes; GitHub Pages serves `main:/docs` with no build step.
- Content:
  - `content/pages/*.md`: About, Experience, and the six expertise pages (front matter at the top; `order` sets nothing yet, `TOPICS` in `build.py` sets the order).
  - `data/profile_data.py`: home-page lede, standing line, selected work, and organisations worked with.
  - `data/publications.py`: keep in step with the project doc "Publication register - Okto Gandhi". Every item carries a DOI (checked on Crossref or DataCite and at doi.org) or a working URL; topic tags are listed at the top of the file.
  - `data/media.py`: press coverage, recognition, and talks. Link only pages that load; use a Wayback Machine copy when the original is gone.
  - `data/redirects.py`: old WordPress addresses and where they now point (generated as small redirect pages).
  - `content/posts/` (writing; the Writing page, nav item, and RSS feed appear only when this folder has posts), `content/unpublished/` (not built).
- Before publishing: take screenshots (desktop and a real 375 px mobile viewport) and look at them; check every fact against Okto's own sources (Master Experience Register and Publication Register in the Consultancy folder, LinkedIn About). Do not invent findings from paper titles: expertise pages state what each piece of work did and found, from the abstract or the full text, without naming the paper (the list at the bottom of each page does that).
- Tone (Okto, 18 November 2026): low-key. Okto works full time at QEERI, so the site must not read as a consultancy pitch. Show the work and its evidence (what it found, where it was applied, publications, media) and let consultancies, development banks, and project owners infer capability from it. No "I help clients" lines, strengths or "what I bring" sections, big number tiles, services, rates, or availability. The list of organisations he has worked with stays (home and Experience pages), as a record of past work (Okto, 18 November 2026). Expertise pages end with "Questions I help answer" (approved by Okto: it does not signal that he is looking for outside work). Contact line: "To discuss a study, a collaboration, a talk, or a media request" (Okto, 18 November 2026).
- Keep off the site: live assignments (e.g. SCOPE-IF), client reports and internal tools, unpublished SERIS floating PV database figures, and anything in the QEERI IP register (`1 Projects/QEERI/QEERI_IP_Register_v1.md`). Describe QEERI work only at the level already public (PV, storage, and grid technologies in desert conditions; principal investigator on projects on resilient PV for arid environments and on grid technologies).
- Voice: Okto's report voice. British English, Oxford comma, no em dashes, no hype or superlatives, numbers stated plainly, explanations hedged, acronyms defined at first use. Never "I was first author of".
- Design: palette and type are defined in `static/css/site.css`. The amber clear-sky curve was removed at Okto's request (18 November 2026); the "OG" monogram is the mark. Portrait: Okto's LinkedIn photo (`Professional photos/Okto CS_0550_cropped.jpg`).
- Discoverability: `build.py` writes JSON-LD (Person, ProfilePage, breadcrumbs, publications with DOIs, media), `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` (search and AI crawlers allowed), `llms.txt`, and `llms-full.txt`. Keep the Person `sameAs` list in step with Okto's profiles.
