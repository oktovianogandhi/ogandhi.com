# ogandhi.com: notes for Claude

Okto Gandhi's personal website. Claude builds and maintains it; Okto approves content and every publish.

- Build: `pip install markdown` (if missing), then `python3 build.py` (Python 3.9+; keep f-string expressions free of backslashes). It rewrites `docs/`. Always rebuild and commit `docs/` together with source changes; GitHub Pages serves `main:/docs` with no build step.
- Content:
  - `content/pages/*.md`: About, Experience, and the six expertise pages (front matter at the top; `order` sets nothing yet, `TOPICS` in `build.py` sets the order).
  - `data/profile_data.py`: home-page lede, standing line, selected work, and organisations worked with.
  - `data/publications.py`: keep in step with the project doc "Publication register - Okto Gandhi". Every item carries a DOI (checked on Crossref or DataCite and at doi.org) or a working URL; topic tags are listed at the top of the file.
  - `data/media.py`: press coverage, recognition, and talks. Link only pages that load; use a Wayback Machine copy when the original is gone.
  - `data/redirects.py`: old WordPress addresses and where they now point (generated as small redirect pages).
  - `content/posts/` (writing; the Writing page, nav item, and RSS feed appear only when this folder has posts), `content/unpublished/` (not built).
- Before publishing: take screenshots (desktop and a real 375 px mobile viewport) and look at them; check every fact against Okto's own sources (Master Experience Register and Publication Register in the Consultancy folder, LinkedIn About). Do not invent findings from paper titles: expertise pages state what each piece of work did and found, from the abstract or the full text, without naming the paper (the list at the bottom of each page does that).
- Tone (Okto, 18 November 2026): low-key. Okto works full time at QEERI, so the site must not read as a consultancy pitch. Show the work and its evidence (what it found, where it was applied, publications, media) and let consultancies, development banks, and project owners infer capability from it. No "I help clients" lines, strengths or "what I bring" sections, big number tiles, services, rates, or availability. The list of organisations he has worked with stays (home and Experience pages), as a record of past work (Okto, 18 November 2026). Expertise pages end with "Questions I help answer" (approved by Okto: it does not signal that he is looking for outside work). Contact line: "To discuss a study, a collaboration, a talk, or a media request" (Okto, 18 November 2026).
- Keep off the site: live assignments (e.g. SCOPE-IF), the contents and figures of client reports and internal tools, unpublished SERIS floating PV database figures, and anything in the QEERI IP register (`1 Projects/QEERI/QEERI_IP_Register_v1.md`). Describe QEERI work only at the level already public (PV, storage, and grid technologies in desert conditions; principal investigator on projects on resilient PV for arid environments and on grid technologies).
- Date the GIZ and PLN work "2023 to 2026", not "since 2023", so it does not read as running alongside the QEERI role (Okto, 22 November 2026; the site and QEERI-context bios only, CVs keep "to present").
- Completed client work may be described at task level, without figures, site names, draft prices, or programme sizes: e.g. the financial model built for MEMR to test draft benchmark prices for PV, PV with battery storage, and stand-alone storage, and the floating PV carbon finance estimates (Okto, 20 November 2026).
- Voice: Okto's report voice. British English, Oxford comma, no em dashes, no hype or superlatives, numbers stated plainly, explanations hedged. Define every abbreviation at its first use on each page (each page may be the reader's first). Never "I was first author of".
- Modelling and simulation results are written with "can", "could", "would", or "has the potential to" unless the sentence already says it is a model or simulation; never make a modelled result read as implemented (Okto, 20 November 2026).
- Search wording: titles, `seo_title` front matter, and descriptions use the nouns development banks, consultancies, and agencies use in their calls (floating solar PV, battery energy storage systems (BESS), grid integration, ancillary services, grid codes, feasibility studies, tariffs, PPA, LCOE, VRE forecasting, mini-grids, rural electrification; "Viet Nam"), as topic names, never as self-labels.
- Design: palette and type are defined in `static/css/site.css`. The amber clear-sky curve was removed at Okto's request (18 November 2026); the "OG" monogram is the mark and the favicon (bump the `?v=` on the icon links when it changes). No coloured or grey bars down the left of text blocks (Okto: they look machine-made). Display name "Dr Oktoviano Gandhi" in the header, home heading, and page titles; structured data keeps `name` plus `honorificPrefix`. Links to other sites open in a new tab (`external_links()` in `build.py`). Atkinson Hyperlegible draws a slashed zero, so digits in sans text come from the system sans ("OG Sans Digits" in `site.css`); keep that when changing fonts. The Expertise menu opens on hover or keyboard focus on devices with a mouse. Portrait: Okto's LinkedIn photo (`Professional photos/Okto CS_0550_cropped.jpg`).
- Discoverability: `build.py` writes JSON-LD (Person, ProfilePage, breadcrumbs, publications with DOIs, media), `sitemap.xml`, `robots.txt` (search and AI crawlers allowed), `llms.txt`, and `llms-full.txt`. Keep the Person `sameAs` list in step with Okto's profiles (LinkedIn, Google Scholar, ORCID, Scopus 57192808691, Wikidata Q142228222, HBKU staff page). The Wikidata item was created on 20 November 2026 from Okto's account; update it when his employer or roles change.
- Indonesian version: one summary page at `/id/` (`content/pages/id.md`), linked from the home page, with hreflang links (en, id, x-default) on those two pages only. Keep its facts in step with About and Experience; Indonesian terms: PLTS, PLTS terapung, PLTS atap, BESS, PJBL, harga patokan, Kementerian ESDM (Okto, 20 November 2026).
- Builds are stable: `build.py` compares each page with the previous build in `docs/` and keeps unchanged pages, their `dateModified`, and their sitemap `lastmod`. Build from the deployed `docs/` (reset to `origin/main` first), and upload only the files `git status` shows as changed.
- Analytics: Cloudflare Web Analytics (cookie-free), set to "Enable with JS Snippet installation" because the domain is DNS-only; the beacon is `CF_BEACON` in `build.py`, and the footer says visits are counted without cookies. Dashboard: Cloudflare, Analytics, Web analytics, ogandhi.com (20 November 2026).
- After each publish, run `python3 indexnow.py` (or with the changed paths) once ogandhi.com serves the new version; it notifies Bing and other IndexNow engines. The key file lives in `static/` and must stay at the site root.
