Oktoviano Gandhi

I work on solar PV, battery storage and their integration into power systems, mostly in Southeast Asia.

I am a Scientist at the Qatar Environment and Energy Research Institute (QEERI), Hamad Bin Khalifa University. Before that, I spent eleven years at the Solar Energy Research Institute of Singapore (SERIS), National University of Singapore, and co-founded Alva Energi, a solar company in Indonesia.

What I work on

Each topic page summarises the questions I have worked on and links to the papers and reports behind them.

  • Floating PV

    Where PV on reservoirs, lakes and the sea makes sense, what it costs, and how to design it.

  • Grid integration of PV and battery storage

    What happens to a power system as PV grows, and how batteries, inverters and forecasts can keep it stable.

  • PV performance in hot climates

    How heat, humidity, dust and sunlight change what PV produces, and which technologies suit which climate.

  • Southeast Asian power systems and energy access

    Indonesia's power sector, rural electrification and island grids, from village mini-grids to national regulation.
