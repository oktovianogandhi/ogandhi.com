Southeast Asian power systems and energy access

Indonesia's power sector, rural electrification and island grids, from village mini-grids to national regulation.

I have worked in Indonesia since 2017, first on rural electrification, then on rooftop PV and power sector studies. Much of my work elsewhere in the region has been funded by development banks and donors.

What I have worked on

  • Remote and island grids. Many remote Indonesian islands rely on diesel generators. With colleagues at SERIS, I worked on how to size and introduce PV and batteries step by step in diesel-powered systems, including A diesel replacement strategy for off-grid systems based on progressive introduction of PV and batteries: An Indonesian case study (Applied Energy, 2018).
  • A book on remote areas. With Professor Dipti Srinivasan, I edited Sustainable Energy Solutions for Remote Areas in the Tropics (Springer, 2020), in which practitioners describe solutions that have been applied in remote communities, their impacts, and how to make them last.
  • Alva Energi. I co-founded Alva Energi in 2017. It has installed more than 800 kWp of solar PV across 18 cities and regencies in Indonesia.
  • Regulation. I was one of three main authors of two studies for GIZ and Indonesia's Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources, on ancillary services and on a regulatory framework and business model for battery energy storage systems. I have also worked with PLN on ancillary services, demand response and renewable energy forecasting.
  • Feasibility studies in the region and beyond. At SERIS, I carried out feasibility studies for more than 7 GWp of PV and 1 GWh of battery storage. Studies I worked on were funded by ADB, the World Bank, the Inter-American Development Bank and USAID, in countries including the Philippines, Viet Nam, Sri Lanka, Mexico, Tuvalu and Suriname.

Questions this work helps answer

  • How can an island grid move from diesel to PV and batteries without losing reliability?
  • What does Indonesia's regulatory framework need for ancillary services and battery storage?
  • What makes an energy access project last after the installers leave?
