Expertise

What I work on: what the work found, where it has been applied, and the publications behind it.

  • Floating solar PV

    Where floating PV makes sense, what it costs compared with ground-mounted PV, and how to take a reservoir, lagoon, or coastal site from screening to feasibility.

  • Battery energy storage (BESS)

    Where batteries add value in a power system, how to size and dispatch them, and how regulation, contracts, and prices can make storage projects bankable.

  • Grid integration, ancillary services, and regulation

    How much solar PV and battery storage a power system can take, which integration measures cost least, and how regulation and markets can make them happen.

  • Solar forecasting and its value

    What a better solar forecast is worth to markets, system operators, and plant owners, and how to produce forecasts that quantify their own uncertainty.

  • PV performance and technology choice

    Which modules, mounting structures, and trackers give the lowest cost of electricity at a given site, and how climate, albedo, and dust change the answer.

  • Feasibility and techno-economics

    Levelised costs, tariffs, and financial returns for PV and battery projects, and the engineering assumptions that drive them.

  • Off-grid, island, and hybrid power systems

    Moving diesel-powered island and remote grids to solar PV and batteries without losing reliability, and keeping village power systems working for years after installation.
