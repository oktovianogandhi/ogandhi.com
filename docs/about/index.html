About

I work on solar PV, battery storage, and their integration into power systems, mostly in Southeast Asia. My work covers feasibility studies, grid integration and ancillary services, power sector regulation and tariffs, and training for utilities and regulators.

I am a Scientist at the Qatar Environment and Energy Research Institute (QEERI), Hamad Bin Khalifa University, in Doha, where I am a principal investigator for projects on resilient PV systems for arid environments and on grid technologies.

Before that, I spent eleven years at the Solar Energy Research Institute of Singapore (SERIS), National University of Singapore (NUS). There I worked on feasibility studies for more than 7 GWp of PV and 1 GWh of battery storage, led the PV grid integration working group of a solar forecasting programme for the Energy Market Authority and Singapore Power, and maintained the SERIS Global Floating PV Database (about 1,700 projects in 66 countries).

In Indonesia, I have worked with GIZ and the Ministry of Energy and Mineral Resources on ancillary services, the regulatory framework for battery storage, rooftop PV hosting capacity, and PV and battery tariffs, and with PT PLN (Persero) on ancillary services, demand response, and renewable energy forecasting. Other studies were funded by ADB, the World Bank, the Inter-American Development Bank, and USAID, in countries including the Philippines, Viet Nam, Sri Lanka, Mexico, Tuvalu, and Suriname. I also co-founded Alva Energi, which has installed more than 800 kWp of solar PV across 18 cities and regencies in Indonesia.

I am Indonesian, lived in Singapore for most of the past twenty years, and work in English, Indonesian, and Malay.

Education

  • PhD, National University of Singapore (2019). Thesis: Reactive power support using photovoltaic systems: techno-economic analysis and implementation algorithms, published in the Springer Theses series. Valedictorian of my PhD cohort.
  • MPhys (Master of Physics), Hertford College, University of Oxford (2015), specialising in atmospheric physics and condensed matter physics.

Research record

More than 50 peer-reviewed publications and two books with Springer Nature, with more than 3,000 citations and an h-index of 27 on Google Scholar (October 2026). I have published in journals including Nature Reviews Clean Technology, Joule, Energy & Environmental Science, and Renewable and Sustainable Energy Reviews, and I review for journals including Applied Energy, IEEE Transactions on Power Systems, IEEE Transactions on Smart Grid, IEEE Transactions on Sustainable Energy, and Solar Energy.
